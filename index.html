EDITABLE PROFILE
Starting values are examples, not a verified fee schedule. Enter rates from your seller account. FBA fees are entered per product; enter the final fee including any applicable surcharge. Monthly subscriptions are allocated through storage / overhead per unit.

Sales VAT is deducted from VAT-inclusive revenue. Fee VAT only adds a cost when non-recoverable. For Shopify Payments, keep the additional platform transaction rate at 0%; for an external gateway, enter both gateway and applicable platform fees. Minimum commission applies to referral / commission only. The threshold model applies its rate to the entire order; use a flat negotiated effective rate for progressive fee schedules.
